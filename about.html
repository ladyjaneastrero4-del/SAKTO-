About SAKTO

Purpose

SAKTO ("just right" in Filipino) is a student budgeting and local discovery website. Many students run out of allowance before the week ends; SAKTO makes spending visible and points to affordable places.

Objectives

  • Help students record and understand their expenses.
  • Encourage saving through simple weekly and monthly logs.
  • Show student-friendly places that fit a chosen price range.

Intended users

College and senior high school students managing a limited allowance.

Built with

HTML5, CSS3 (Flexbox, Grid, media queries) and vanilla JavaScript. Data is stored in your browser using localStorage. No frameworks are used; fonts (Outfit, Inter) come from Google Fonts.
